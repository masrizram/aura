# AURA Independent Engineering Assurance

### Your AI agent wrote the code.
### Can you prove the repository is defensible?

---

AI coding agents deliver software faster than ever. But velocity creates a new
problem: **who independently verifies what the agent produced?**

AURA is an autonomous engineering assurance engine that audits, remediates,
independently verifies, and drives repositories toward a defensible state —
through repeated evidence-backed cycles until convergence gates are met or a
genuine human blocker is reached.

---

## What you get

- Full-spectrum repository audit (architecture, correctness, security, data
  integrity, reliability, performance, testing, observability, operations,
  maintainability, documentation)
- Adversarial audit from 6 hostile personas (attacker, incident, dependency,
  hostile input, scale, future maintainer)
- Automated remediation of material findings
- Independent verification with tooling evidence
- Regression check across all cycles
- 11-gate convergence evaluation
- Executive summary + findings + evidence + final recommendation
